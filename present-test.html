Present Simple vs Present Continuous

🧠 10 вопросов

Проверь себя

Present Simple — привычки и факты. Present Continuous — действия, происходящие сейчас.

Simple

Mario plays every day.

usually, often, every day
Continuous

Mario is playing now.

now, look, at the moment
